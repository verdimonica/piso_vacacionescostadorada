About this place

Spacious and well located apartment on Carrer Xuquer, in the heart of Segur de Calafell. Just 3 minutes on foot from the train station and 5 minutes from the seafront, where you can enjoy the wonderful and quiet atmosphere of one of the Costa Daurada's most charming towns.
